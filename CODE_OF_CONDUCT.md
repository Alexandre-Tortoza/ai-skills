# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should remain useful, professional and safe for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, evidence-based review, respect for other contributors' time, and criticism focused on code, decisions and outcomes rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, or sustained personal attacks.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, project-maintainer interactions and other spaces where someone is participating on behalf of `ai-skills`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement should be proportional, documented when appropriate and focused on restoring a productive project environment.

For sensitive reports that should not be made public, use a private repository contact channel. Until a dedicated conduct-reporting address exists, the repository's private GitHub reporting channel may be used with a clear note that the report concerns conduct rather than a software vulnerability.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and is informed by common open-source community codes of conduct, including the Contributor Covenant principles.
